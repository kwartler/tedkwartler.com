About

Data, business acumen, and ethics, in one stack.

I’m Ted Kwartler, Managing Director of Accenture’s North American Responsible AI practice, where I help organizations deploy generative AI and machine learning that is transparent, ethical, and effective in the real world.

I was an early employee at DataRobot, serving as field Chief Technology Officer focused on generative AI and large language models, and helping set product strategy for explainable and ethical AI. Before that I built and led data-driven teams at Amazon and Liberty Mutual Insurance.

I teach what I practice. As an instructor at Harvard Extension School and faculty at GSERM / the University of St. Gallen, I’ve introduced thousands of students to data mining, text analytics, and applied AI. I’m the author of several R and analytics books and a contributor to the World Economic Forum on the future of responsible AI.
